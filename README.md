# 📊 LEGO Sales Business Intelligence Dashboard

## 📖 Overview

This project presents a complete Business Intelligence solution for analyzing LEGO product sales using Power BI dashboards, forecasting, and business analytics.

Developed as part of the **Business Systems** course at **Al-Hussein Technical University (HTU)**, the project demonstrates how marketplace data can support pricing strategies, marketing campaigns, inventory planning, and executive decision-making.

The solution combines data collection, visualization, forecasting, and business recommendations to transform raw sales data into actionable insights.

---

# 🎯 Project Objectives

The project aims to:

- Analyze sales performance across multiple LEGO product categories.
- Evaluate the impact of pricing and discounts on customer demand.
- Forecast future product demand.
- Support inventory planning.
- Optimize pricing and promotional strategies.
- Enable evidence-based business decision making.

---

# 🧸 Products Analyzed

The project compares three LEGO product categories:

- LEGO Botanical Collection
- LEGO Classic Medium Creative Brick Box
- LEGO Star Wars Imperial Star Destroyer

These products represent different customer segments and pricing strategies, allowing meaningful comparisons across premium, creative, and franchise-based products. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

# 💼 Business Insights

Key findings include:

- Classic products responded strongly to discounts.
- Botanical products maintained stable premium pricing.
- Star Wars products showed demand influenced by franchise-related events.
- Forecasting improved inventory planning.
- Pricing strategies should differ by product category.

These insights were translated into practical recommendations for marketing, pricing, and inventory management. :contentReference[oaicite:4]{index=4}
